ABOUT

Built for focused minds

TokiSpirit started as a simple idea: what if studying felt less like a chore and more like an adventure? We blended the Pomodoro technique with pixel art aesthetics and a light reward system — and TokiSpirit was born.

◆

Our Mission

To make deep focus accessible and enjoyable for every student and learner — regardless of experience, background, or study style.

⏱

The Method

We're built on the Pomodoro Technique — 25 minutes of focus, 5 minutes of rest. It's been proven to improve concentration and reduce burnout.

🎨

The Aesthetic

Your study space matters. That's why we obsess over every wallpaper, animation, and pixel — so your workspace feels as good as your progress.

THE STORY

Why we built this

Existing productivity apps felt cold and corporate. Spreadsheets. Harsh timers. Minimalist to the point of joyless. We wanted something different — an app that acknowledged that studying is hard, and tried to make it a little more fun.

So we built TokiSpirit. Every animated theme was chosen to create a mood. The gold rewards system keeps you motivated. The pixel mascot keeps you company. And the stats dashboard shows you how far you've come.

We're indie developers who love studying and love design. This is our small contribution to the world of focused work.
